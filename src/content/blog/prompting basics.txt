I've been really wanting to understand how AI works, especially how it can come up with sentences to reply. How does it know the nuances of the language and how do conversations become conversations in the original? I dove into this topic and discovered the mathematical science around it. 

The key insight is that LLMs don't store knowledge as paragraphs of text ready to be returned like traditional (non-AI) Google.

They store relationships between concepts as mathematical patterns in a high-dimensional space. Every response is generated by navigating that space and predicting the most probable next token based on everything that came before.

That's why prompting isn't about finding the "right command." It's about providing context that positions the model in the right region of semantic space so it can generate the response you're looking for.
